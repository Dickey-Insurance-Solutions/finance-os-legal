# DIS Finance OS — Privacy Policy

**Effective:** November 13, 2026
**Operator:** Dickey Insurance Solutions, LLC ("DIS"), 26022 Budde Rd Suite A-206, The Woodlands, TX 77380

DIS Finance OS is a private, internal application that DIS uses to understand its own finances. It has no public users and is not offered to customers.

## What data it accesses
- **DIS's own QuickBooks Online accounting data**, read-only: chart of accounts, account balances, transactions and financial reports for DIS's company file. The application requests only the QuickBooks accounting scope and never writes to QuickBooks.
- **Sign-in details for authorized DIS users** (name, work email, and a securely hashed password or Microsoft sign-in), used only to control access.

The application does not collect data from DIS's insurance clients, does not use cookies for advertising or tracking, and does not collect data from the public.

## How it is used
Data is used only to produce internal financial reports, reconciliations and forecasts for DIS management. It is never sold, rented or shared for marketing. Payroll and compensation details, where present, are restricted to the account owner.

## Where it is stored and how it is protected
Data is stored in a private, access-controlled database in the application's hosting environment (Replit). Connections use encrypted transport (HTTPS/TLS). OAuth tokens and other secrets are kept in encrypted secret storage, never in source code. Access is limited to authorized DIS personnel.

## Service providers
DIS uses Intuit (QuickBooks Online) as the data source and Replit as the hosting provider. If AI-assisted analysis is enabled, DIS may send summarized, non-public financial figures to an AI provider under that provider's business terms, within daily and monthly spending caps; this feature is off unless DIS turns it on.

## Retention and deletion
DIS keeps data only as long as needed for its internal financial management. DIS can disconnect QuickBooks at any time (QuickBooks Online → Apps → My Apps), after which no new data is retrieved, and can delete stored data on request of DIS ownership.
